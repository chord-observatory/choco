Instrument

The signal path from the power supplies to the files, top to bottom. Each CRS board receives one polarization of eight dishes and sends every frequency to the switch; the switch sends each frequency stream to one X-engine NIC port. Feeds show their PDB power; links show the live rate (their width too) and their state. Everything here is read from Prometheus, choco's own monitors, kotekan and kotekan's config, every 5 s while the page is open; nothing that changes anything is sent to the devices. The port tables are on the switch page, the servers on nodes and the outlets on power.
